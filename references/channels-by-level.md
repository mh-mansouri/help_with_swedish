## Skill Mapping
- **Listening**: Slow/clear speech → normal speed (Peter SFI normal speech, Oskar dialogues)
- **Reading**: Videos with Swedish subtitles + transcripts (Oskar Patreon/transcripts, UR Play)
- **Writing**: Grammar-focused (Peter SFI), then produce sentences from video content
- **Speaking**: Shadowing + dialogue practice (Swedish Shadowing, Oskar Conversation Practice)
